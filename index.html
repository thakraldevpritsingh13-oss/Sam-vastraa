About Us

Manufacturing identity, one uniform at a time

Founded by Manohar Anand Gulati, SamVastra has spent 4 years building a reputation for reliable, premium bulk garment manufacturing. We began with a simple focus: manufacture garments that hold up to real, everyday use — in classrooms, offices, hospitals and factory floors.

Today that focus has grown into a full bulk manufacturing operation specializing in customized T-shirts, school uniforms, corporate uniforms, industrial uniforms and institutional apparel. Every order — whether 100 pieces or several thousand — moves through the same disciplined process, so the identity our clients trust us with is represented consistently across every garment.
